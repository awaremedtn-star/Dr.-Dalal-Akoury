About JT Foxx

JT Foxx has spent decades building, scaling, and partnering with companies across dozens of countries as a global entrepreneur, investor, and dealmaker. In recent years his focus has shifted toward artificial intelligence, which he sees as the next major shift in how businesses compete. Drawing on his background in growth strategy, branding, and mergers and acquisitions, he founded AI Your Company to help businesses adopt practical AI infrastructure rather than treat it as a side experiment. He has also developed a large-scale proprietary AI system for business applications, which he calls his "9 Figure AI Brain." More recently, he has begun exploring AI-driven robotics — pairing automated systems with physical operations as a further step in how companies might staff and scale.

The Next Chapter: AI

JT Foxx's recent shift toward AI-powered business tools is directly relevant to how Egypt Vision plans to scale. The six-pillar model outlined in the investment thesis — from franchise licensing to digital platforms — depends on exactly the kind of infrastructure his AI Your Company work focuses on: systems that help a growing network of clinics, licensed partners, and trained physicians operate consistently across borders, not just at a single flagship location. His parallel interest in AI-driven robotics and automation points toward the same question Egypt Vision's investment structure is built to answer — how a proven healthcare model gets deployed at franchise scale rather than rebuilt clinic by clinic.
