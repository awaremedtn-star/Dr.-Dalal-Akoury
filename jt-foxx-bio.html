From Stutter to Stage

He grew up in Canada with a pronounced stutter that made public speaking difficult, and has spoken publicly about turning that early struggle into the drive behind his career. He got his start in real estate investing, building a local following in the Chicago area partly through a radio show, and reports having made his first million by age 24. A speaking trip to South Africa marked an early turning point, broadening his focus from real estate alone toward business coaching more generally.

Building an Empire

Foxx founded what became the JT Foxx Organization, offering business and wealth coaching along with mergers-and-acquisitions strategy he brands the "M7A model." His flagship event, Mega Success, grew out of a smaller real-estate-focused gathering into a large annual conference; he also runs related events under the Mega One and Mega Partnering names. Guest lists and testimonials associated with his events and platforms have publicly named a range of entrepreneurs, athletes, and entertainment figures over the years — these associations are as reported by JT Foxx's own organization and media appearances, not independently confirmed here.

The Next Chapter: AI

In recent years Foxx has shifted a significant part of his public focus toward artificial intelligence for business, launching an AI-implementation offering aimed at helping companies adopt AI tools for growth, branding, and marketing. He has also published a series of business-strategy books under the "Business Is War" title, with at least two entries reported to have reached bestseller status on Amazon.
