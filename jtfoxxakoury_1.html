Who Is JT Foxx

What he does, in his own public terms.

JT Foxx describes himself as the world's #1 wealth and business coach — a global entrepreneur, investor and dealmaker who does business across dozens of countries and owns or partners with a large portfolio of companies spanning AI, technology, real estate, hospitality and entertainment.

Publicly, he is best known for three things: connection — his events, among them Mega Success and Mega One, are described as gatherings of billionaires, investors and internationally recognized figures; dealmaking — he built a mergers-and-acquisitions approach he calls the M7A model, focused on scaling and partnering with companies; and capital — he publicly promotes co-investing in and funding deals. More recently he has focused on artificial intelligence for business.
