Why This Partnership Matters

Two Different Strengths, Aimed at the Same Opportunity.

Dr. Akoury’s work is medicine, whole-person healthcare, education, scientific leadership and a mission focused on Egypt. JT Foxx’s work is entrepreneurship, strategic relationships, international capital networks and business development.

Neither strength alone builds an ecosystem. Medical authority without global access stays local. Global access without medical authority has nothing credible to introduce. Together, the two could open a serious conversation about what Egypt builds next — without either party guaranteeing the outcome.

Great ideas rarely fail for lack of ambition. They fail for lack of the right people in the room.
