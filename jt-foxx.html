Who Is JT Foxx

An Entrepreneur Who Connects Capital, Credibility & Relationships

JT Foxx is an internationally recognized business coach, entrepreneur, and investor who works with business owners across dozens of countries. He is widely known for coaching, mergers & acquisitions, and for hosting large international business events that bring together entrepreneurs, executives, and high-profile speakers.

His approach centers on combining four things into one strategy: capital, relationships, credibility, and technology (including AI) — helping entrepreneurs scale faster, execute smarter, and position for larger opportunities.

The JT Foxx × Akoury Connection

A Physician's Mission, Amplified by a Dealmaker's Network

Dr. Dalal Akoury brings something rare: four decades of clinical validation, a vertically integrated platform (AWAREmed · IAMI · MMDP · AWAREmed 360), and a proven, cash-pay integrative-medicine model. What a network like JT Foxx's adds is reach, dealmaking discipline, and access to serious capital and relationships.

Together, the combination is straightforward: a credible, ready-to-deploy healthcare system on one side, and the coaching, M&A know-how, and global relationships to help scale it responsibly on the other. For a market like Egypt and the wider Middle East, that pairing is designed to turn vision into structured, investable opportunity — through separate, formal agreements where and when they make sense.
