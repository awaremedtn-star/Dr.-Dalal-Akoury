The Connector & The Vision

A global dealmaker, an Egyptian vision, and a conversation worth having.

Dr. Dalal Akoury's vision for Egypt calls for a rare kind of partner — someone whose network crosses continents and whose business is opening doors. JT Foxx is one of the world's most connected entrepreneurs. This page explains who he is, what he does, and why a collaboration could matter for Egyptian enterprise.

The Two Names

Vision needs a connector.

The Vision

Dr. Dalal Akoury

Physician and founder, building a healthcare and legacy vision rooted in Egypt.

The Connector

JT Foxx

Global entrepreneur, investor and dealmaker operating across dozens of countries.

Great ideas rarely fail for lack of ambition. They fail for lack of the right people in the room. Dr. Akoury brings a vision for Egypt — in healthcare, development and legacy. A connector like JT Foxx represents the kind of global reach that can carry that vision to partners, capital and platforms far beyond Egypt's borders.

Who Is JT Foxx

What he does, in his own public terms.

JT Foxx describes himself as the world's #1 wealth and business coach — a global entrepreneur, investor and dealmaker who does business across dozens of countries and owns or partners with a large portfolio of companies spanning AI, technology, real estate, hospitality and entertainment.

Publicly, he is best known for three things: connection — his events, among them Mega Success and Mega One, are described as gatherings of billionaires, investors and internationally recognized figures; dealmaking — he built a mergers-and-acquisitions approach he calls the M7A model, focused on scaling and partnering with companies; and capital — he publicly promotes co-investing in and funding deals. More recently he has focused on artificial intelligence for business.

Why It Matters for Egypt

What a global connector can bring to Egyptian enterprise.

Egypt has world-class opportunity and a proven base of home-grown enterprise. What global connectors add is reach — access to international capital, partners and platforms that turn national opportunity into global scale.

01

Access to Capital

Introductions to international investors and funding structures that complement Egypt's own capital.

02

Global Partners

Relationships across dozens of markets — the kind that open doors for Egyptian ventures abroad.

03

Deal Structuring

Experience scaling, partnering and acquiring — turning promising ventures into larger enterprises.

04

Global Stage

Platforms and events that put Egyptian opportunity in front of an international audience.

The benefit is to Egyptian enterprise broadly — its entrepreneurs, its investors, and the opportunities its economy now offers to the world. This is about widening the circle, not any single arrangement.
