Her Story

From Alexandria to a System Built to Outlast Her

I left Egypt to learn from the world. I return because the world has taught me enough.

— Dr. Dalal Akoury

Dr. Akoury was born in Alexandria before leaving Egypt to train and practice in the United States. Over more than forty years as an Emory- and St. Jude-trained pediatric oncologist, she cared for children and families through some of life's hardest moments — an experience that taught her how fragile, and how precious, a single life can be. That experience became the foundation for AWAREmed, the integrative healthcare system she built from the ground up. Now, having reached the kind of success where significance matters more than scale, she is bringing that system home to Egypt — not to open another clinic, but to build something that will outlast her.
