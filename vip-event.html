The Vision

One Room. One Evening. A Different Trajectory.

Some rooms change the course of things. This is designed to be one of them — an evening where the people who build, fund, and reimagine the future sit together as equals. Not a conference, not a pitch. A quiet convergence of capital, conviction, and intelligence, gathered in Egypt to explore what becomes possible when the right people finally share the same table.

The Three Registers of the Evening

Educational. Transformational. Connective.

Three registers shape every moment of the evening — the reasons this gathering is worth a guest's most valuable resource: their presence.

◉

Educational

Frontier thinking on where the world is turning — the edge of AI, capital, and cross-border opportunity, shared by the people who are actually building it. Guests leave knowing something they could not have read.

△

Transformational

The premise is that international business can be done better — more human, more legacy-minded, more enduring. The evening is designed to change how the room thinks about where and how they build next.

❖

Connective

Introductions between people at this altitude are rare and deliberate. Anchored by JT Foxx's global network across 50+ countries, the connections made here are chosen, not accidental.
