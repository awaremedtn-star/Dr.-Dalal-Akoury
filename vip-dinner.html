The Vision

One Table. One Evening. A Different Trajectory.

Some rooms change the course of things. This is designed to be one of them — a single table where the people who build, fund, and reimagine the future sit together as equals. Not a conference, not a banquet. A quiet convergence of capital, conviction, and intelligence, gathered in Egypt to explore what becomes possible when the right few finally share the same table.
